# Compliance request

Source: a client-supplied compliance request, received in two parts on 2026-09-30 and 2026-10-02. The organization is anonymized as **Acme**, a fictional organization. The request is paraphrased below in neutral terms. No additional architecture, policy or audit information was supplied.

## Scope

Acme's GRC function needs continuous auditing and monitoring to support its compliance program.

- Drive technology compliance activities, including external audit and service readiness.
- Draft compliance reports that summarize objectives and key findings, and work with teams to remediate those findings.
- Identify internal control issues, define them clearly and identify root causes.
- Coordinate with GRC staff in multiple locations.
- Communicate with business and technical staff, including management.

## Environment and frameworks

- Cloud environments: AWS, Azure and Google Cloud.
- Frameworks in scope: SOC 2, ISO 27001, HITRUST, HIPAA and PCI DSS.
- Core IT processes in scope: SDLC, identity and access management, and backup and disaster recovery.
- Automated control testing is the expected delivery method.

## Supplementary priorities

The second part of the request set clearer priorities. They drive the [deeper projects](projects/README.md).

- Most of the work is building control automation use cases.
- Evaluate and test remediation and audit processes.
- The focus is GRC engineering: automated controls and configuration.
- Evaluate whether a control is designed correctly, and test that evidence data reaches the control.
- Framework priority: SOC 2 first, then ISO standards, then HIPAA. PCI DSS is handled by a separate team and has lower priority here.
